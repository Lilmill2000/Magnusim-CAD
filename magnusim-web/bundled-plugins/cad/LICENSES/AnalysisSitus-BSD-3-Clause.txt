Analysis Situs 2026.1.1 (commit 3c3ec3ff077ef71eaee39dc062e01aada475808f,
https://gitlab.com/ssv/AnalysisSitus): the part of its asiAlgo library, and of the
Active Data interfaces asiAlgo uses, that the CAD plugin's kernel build compiles.
Those files are in kernel-build/asi/upstream/ exactly as published (169 files);
kernel-build/asi/asi-occt79-wasm.patch is our port of 13 of them to OCCT 7.9.3
and WebAssembly, and a speed change to a 14th (see kernel-build/SOURCES.md).
